## 语言与素材处理

- **对白**：现有 take 是中文对白。国际发布两个选择：① 中文原片 + 英文字幕（AI 视频圈看的是表演质量，字幕完全可接受，可先发）；② 重渲英文对白版（追求传播上限时做；台词英译时"再等一天"的轻重节奏要重新设计，不是直译）。建议：**先发字幕版试水，数据好再投英文重渲**。

## 钓饵与分层机制（每条固定动作）

- **解析免费给足**；完整 prompt 用 "reply / DM for the full prompt" 抓联系人（X 圈惯例，接受度高）。
- 分层信号：要 prompt 的 → **需求侧**入池；来聊调度细节（why no reverse shot / push-in speed）的 → **供给侧**入池。
- ②的 CTA 是关键实验（人肉 MVP）：**"Tell me your scenario in the replies — I'll fork this shot for a few of you."** 回应量与质量 = remix 需求的第一手证据，直接写进 A3 结论。
- Banodoco / Reddit 另有社区礼仪：先正常参与再发自己的东西，不带营销味；赛事获奖者用 DM 逐个建联（夸具体镜头，不发模板私信）。
- 全部入池者记入跟踪表（渠道/类型/状态/意向）。

## ① Breakdown 条 · X thread 文案（英文，可直接用）

**Tweet 1（挂片）**
> This 30s AI scene isn't a lucky roll.
> She's about to end a relationship she's still in — and the model *acts* it.
> 5 directing decisions that made it work 🧵

**Tweet 2 — One emotional fact**
> The whole scene runs on a single fact:
> "She still loves him. She's decided to leave. She's forcing herself to say it."
> Heartbreak, guilt, tenderness — all *consequences* of that fact, not a stack of emotion keywords. Stacked emotions is why most AI acting fails.

**Tweet 3 — Restraint over breakdown**
> Display policy: she holds it in. The scene ends *before* she breaks.
> Models act "barely holding it together" far better than sobbing. The cracks — tears pooling but not falling, a smile that doesn't land — that's where the acting lives.

**Tweet 4 — Write the choking into the script**
> "I really… can't do this anymore."
> The pauses, the stalled words, the last line delivered the quietest — none of that is dead air. It's *written performance*. The lightest line carries the heaviest information.

**Tweet 5 — The camera does exactly one thing**
> Over-the-shoulder puts you in the seat of the person being left.
> Then one slow push-in, the whole take. The push *is* her forcing herself to speak, externalized. No coverage, no orbit. One motivated move = the model's most reliable move.

**Tweet 6 — End unresolved**
> She doesn't turn. Doesn't leave. Doesn't wipe the tear.
> Hold on the unresolved emotion, cut before collapse. The two seconds where *you* ache for her — that's the share button.

**Tweet 7（CTA）**
> Full prompt (verbatim, Seedance 30s): reply "prompt" or DM.
> Which decision matters most? And if you want the ablation cut — same prompt with one decision removed, watch it fall apart — say so 👇

## ② Fork 条 · X thread 文案

**Tweet 1（双屏对比挂片）**
> These are two different films. Same actor direction, same camera, same beats.
> I only changed the character and the dialogue.
> This is one shot, forked. 🧵

**Tweet 2 — the diff**
> Changed: gender, four lines of dialogue (his version hangs the breakup on "tomorrow's our anniversary").
> Unchanged: night interior, cold light, OTS framing, the slow push-in, the 4-beat spine (holding back → first line → choking → quietest line last), ending before collapse.

**Tweet 3 — the point**
> The reusable asset in AI video isn't the prompt text.
> It's the *direction* underneath: one emotional fact, beat timing, one motivated camera move, an unresolved ending.
> Lock the direction, and characters, lines, events become slots you swap.

**Tweet 4 — the word**
> I've started calling this **forking a shot**. Like code: the structure is proven, you make it tell *your* story.

**Tweet 5（CTA·关键实验）**
> What would you fork this shot into?
> Long-distance call? A hospital goodbye? Best friends splitting up?
> Drop your scenario below — I'll fork a runnable version for a few of them. Both full prompts (with the diff highlighted): DM "fork".
